\# Purpose



This document defines the principles that guide strategic, architectural, technical, and business decisions within Moltrix.



Rather than relying on trends or personal preference, decisions are made using consistent, evidence-based principles.



\---



\# Decision-Making Principles



\## 1. Business Value First



Every decision must contribute measurable business value.



\---



\## 2. Simplicity Before Complexity



Choose the simplest solution that satisfies the requirements.



\---



\## 3. Security Is Non-Negotiable



Security requirements cannot be compromised for convenience.



\---



\## 4. Cloud-Agnostic Thinking



Solutions should avoid unnecessary vendor lock-in unless a clear business advantage exists.



\---



\## 5. Automation Whenever Practical



Repetitive work should be automated whenever it improves quality, consistency, or efficiency.



\---



\## 6. Evidence Over Opinion



Architectural decisions should be supported by data, testing, documentation, and measurable outcomes.



\---



\## 7. Scalability by Design



Solutions should be designed with future growth in mind.



\---



\## 8. Maintainability Matters



Long-term maintainability is as important as initial implementation speed.



\---



\## 9. Continuous Improvement



Every project should leave the platform better than it was before.



\---



\## 10. Responsible Innovation



New technologies should be adopted thoughtfully, balancing innovation with reliability, security, and business needs.



\---



\# Our Commitment



Moltrix is committed to making transparent, consistent, and evidence-based decisions that create sustainable value for customers and the platform.
